03 / The inspiration Where it all comes from

What is our
inspiration?

Elemental is the brainchild of Dany Duncan, who has more than 40 years’ experience working in outdoor pursuits in the UK and overseas.

He started out running watersports centres and children’s activity holidays every summer, then teaching skiing every winter. That included coaching paralympic skiers and competing in ski mountaineering. In 2006 he set up Elemental UK, a company devoted to adventurous activities in the mountains and on the water.

The first base was Swanpool Beach in Falmouth, a naturally sheltered bay that’s perfect for kayaking and sailing. Beach centres in Newquay followed in 2011, and in 2022 came the first hotel, Elemental Surf Lodge, with an on site surf school for the waves at world famous Fistral. Winters still see Dany and the team heading to the Alps, running ski trips to France, Italy, Switzerland, Austria and beyond.

Twenty years on, Elemental has an established band of highly qualified, DBS checked staff, and has welcomed thousands of people to the beach and the mountains, whatever their age and skill level, nurturing talent and building confidence.
