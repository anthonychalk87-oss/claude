About the Expo What · When · Where

Three days, one town, every board on it.

24 · 25 · 26 July 2026 / Newquay, TR7 2SL

The UK Surf & Skate Expo brings surf and skate cultures together for one weekend in their spiritual home, the home of British surfing, Newquay.

Across three venues, a 7,000 m² Expo Village with a purpose built skate park, an 800 seat Expo Hall presented by YETI, and the town’s beaches and bowls, the weekend gathers the brands, athletes, filmmakers and riders who shape the culture in the UK.

Free to wander, ticketed for the talks, films and awards. The expo runs Friday through Sunday with the Wavelength UK Surfer Awards on Friday night and the European Surf Film Festival on Saturday night.

01 / Expo Village

The Village

A 7,000 m² field of marquees, outdoor pitches, food and drink, a 40′ outdoor film screen and a purpose built skate park. Free entry, all ages, all weekend.

Why stay with us Values we share

A lodge built for the people the Expo is built for.

The Expo brings surf and skate together for a weekend. We do it every week of the year. Here’s why our values line up, and why staying with us makes the whole weekend easier, friendlier and a lot more fun.

06 / Reasons
01 / Community

Housemates, not guests

You’ll be sharing breakfast with skaters, longboarders, foilers and first time riders from across the UK and beyond. Conversations carry over from the Expo and into the lodge Games Room.

02 / Built for boards

Wax it, ride it, dry it

Secure board storage, a proper wetsuit drying area and outdoor rinse showers. Bring the surfboard, bring the skateboard, bring both. The lodge knows what to do with them.

03 / Location

Everything on your doorstep

Fistral Beach is a 10 minute walk. Great Western Beach is 20. Concrete Waves skatepark is 25 minutes on foot. The Expo Village a 35 minute walk or a short ride. No car needed all weekend.

04 / Daybreak

Dawn patrol included

The Expo opens at 9. Surf opens earlier. Coffee from 7:30, swell report on the wall, a lodge surf coach on hand if you want a private lesson on the water before, during or after the day.

05 / Run by riders

Local knowledge, on tap

Our team surfs, skates and lives here year-round. Ask which break is firing, which Expo session is worth the queue, and where to eat after the awards. Real answers, not a tourist desk.

06 / Fuel

Tea, toast, tide chart

The basics, done well. Tea, coffee, toast, cereal and yogurt every morning. Honest fuel before the Expo Village opens at 9. Tide times and swell report up on the kitchen wall.

Distance & transport Around Newquay

A weekend you can walk.

The lodge sits between the train station and the sea, with the Expo Village a short walk one way and the beaches the other. No car, no taxis, no fuss.
