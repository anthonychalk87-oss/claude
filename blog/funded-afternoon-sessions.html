We’ve secured funding to continue free surf lessons
for local children
who need it most.

Cornwall’s children can now access free surf and beach activities thanks to a three-way partnership between Elemental UK, the Cornwall Community Foundation and the Boardmasters Foundation.

By Anthony Chalk · North Coast Manager, Elemental Surf Lodge
Supported by the Boardmasters Foundation, Jane Hartley’s AVdB Fund & Cornwall Community Foundation

Three-way partnership keeps free surf sessions running for children who are eligible for benefits-related free school meals.

More time in the water, for the families who need it most.

We’re thrilled to announce that Elemental Surf Academy has secured funding for our Time 2 Move sessions at Fistral Beach, thanks to the Boardmasters Foundation, Jane Hartley’s AVdB Fund and the Cornwall Community Foundation.

Now in its sixth year of delivery in partnership with Cornwall Council, our free Time 2 Move programme gives children who are eligible for benefits-related free school meals a proper introduction to the ocean, led by qualified surf and beach coaches, fully safeguarded and DBS-compliant. This summer’s programme covers 16 days, with 35 fully funded morning places available each day directly through Time 2 Move. In previous years Time 2 Move funded a full day, 35 places each in the morning and afternoon, but this year’s allocation covers mornings only.

The holiday programme used to run all day long, but with less funding available this year, sessions were cut to half days. That is not a neutral change. A significant number of families travel for the provision, and for working parents a half-day drop-off and return is not a realistic option. For some, it is the difference between their child coming or not coming at all.

That’s why we went looking for partners. We reached out to Robert Spring, Boardmasters Festival Director, who personally got in touch with the Cornwall Community Foundation, leading to an award of £1,200 from the Boardmasters Foundation and £1,200 from Jane Hartley’s AVdB Fund, both of which are managed by the Cornwall Community Foundation.

Elemental UK is matching the grant pound for pound, putting in a minimum of £2,400 of its own funding, so every penny awarded is doubled to fund 10 fully free afternoon places each day, making a total of 160 child sessions across the 16-day programme. And as an additional commitment on top of that match, Elemental UK is also subsidising 25 further afternoon places each day, so the adventure carries on straight after lunch for as many families as possible. We’re offering the same subsidised afternoon option at our Falmouth watersports centre too.

About Elemental UK Who’s behind it

Working with children is our DNA.

Newquay · Falmouth

Elemental Surf Lodge and Academy are part of Elemental UK, a specialist school tour operator. Every season we welcome thousands of students to our centres in Newquay and Falmouth for watersports residentials and bespoke educational trips.

That’s why the standard never slips: every session is led by qualified surf and beach activity coaches, fully safeguarded and DBS-compliant, the same on a Time 2 Move afternoon as on any school residential.

The programme Holiday Activity & Food

What is Time 2 Move?

Approved provider 2026/27

Time 2 Move is Cornwall’s holiday activity and food programme, run by Cornwall Council, coordinated by Active Cornwall and funded by the Department for Education. It gives children who are eligible for benefits-related free school meals free access to sport, outdoor activity and a healthy meal during the school holidays, because holidays shouldn’t mean going without.

We’re proud to be an approved provider. Our sessions run from Fistral Beach: surfing, bodyboarding and beach games led by our coaches, with a healthy lunch as part of the day. Booking is free through Playwaze.
