07 / FAQ The questions we get

Group booking,
short answers.

Anything else · drop us a line
01 What is the maximum group size? ↓

Group sizes vary by season and room mix. Contact us with your dates and headcount and we’ll come back with the right configuration, plus a whole lodge price if your group is big enough to take the building.

02 Do you offer group discounts? ↓

Every lodge guest gets discounted surf lessons and board hire at the academy next door. For larger group bookings we’ll put together a bespoke package covering accommodation, sessions and any meals you want included.

03 Can you take school groups? ↓

Yes. We’ve been hosting school residentials for twenty years. Our parent company Elemental UK handles the full programme. Accommodation, activities, logistics. Teachers turn up with the kids and we look after the rest. School programmes are here.

04 Is the surf school included in a group booking? ↓

The academy is a separate operation, but lodge guests get discounted lesson and hire rates. We can package accommodation and surf sessions together for groups. Say so when you enquire and we’ll quote the combined price.

05 Where is Elemental Surf Lodge? ↓

61 to 63 Tower Road, Newquay, TR7 1LX. Between Towan and Fistral, a ten minute walk to world famous Fistral Beach and a short walk into town.

06 Do you cater for the group? ↓

For school and retreat groups, yes. Full board with hot breakfasts, packed lunches and proper evening meals served in the dining room. Stag, hen and private groups usually self cater or eat out in town. Either way, the kitchen and dining room are yours to use.
